Nitrogen AI is a chat-first platform for teams planning and evaluating sustainable development projects. Describe a project in plain language and Nitrogen AI builds a structured **project plan**, recommends the **assessments** you need to run, and tracks an ongoing **project status**, all grounded in the evidence you upload.

<Warning>
  Nitrogen AI is a diligence-support tool, not an adviser. It is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or provider of investment, financial, legal, or tax advice. Project status, assessment outputs, and risk-summary memos are AI-assisted drafts — verify them independently before relying on them or sharing them with investors, donors, or other third parties. See the [Terms of Service](https://github.com/nicholasrossano/nitrogen/blob/main/TERMS.md#4-not-investment-financial-legal-or-tax-advice).
</Warning>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your project">
    Start a chat and describe your project in plain language. Nitrogen AI captures this as a project record and infers geography, sector, and other details from your description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload evidence">
    Add supporting documents. The platform indexes them for retrieval so every output cites your actual materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a project plan">
    Nitrogen AI produces a plan organized into pillars, each with concrete work items to track and launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run assessments">
    Launch any assessment from the plan. Each walks through a setup, build, and output flow in a dedicated workspace, producing a DOCX or XLSX export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track project status">
    Nitrogen AI continuously assesses project status across editable categories, so you always know what's healthy and what needs attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>
