title: "Project status"
description: "Project status tracks a project's health across editable categories, each with an AI-computed status, confidence, and rationale."
---

**Project status** gives you an at-a-glance view of how healthy a project is, broken into categories you can customize. Each category is assessed by AI primarily against your project's definition, evidence, and materials. Formal assessment outputs are optional signals that help fill gaps — they are not required for status to run. You can manually override a result when your judgment differs from the model.

## Default categories

Every new project starts with five editable categories:

- **Evidence & credibility** — material claims are backed by traceable sources, with no major contradictions in the project record
- **Technical viability** — the proposed design and modeled outputs are coherent for this site and use case
- **Funding & economics** — cost, revenue, and funding logic hang together and look directionally credible
- **Deployment readiness** — a credible path to build and operate exists, with owners and no unresolved blockers
- **Risk profile** — material risks are identified, owned, and mitigated for the most important items

Each category has an editable **definition** describing what "healthy" means for that category on your project. Definitions are seeded for the defaults, or written by you when you add or edit a category. The info tip on each category shows a short LLM summary derived from that definition; the fuller definition lives in the editor. Internally, Nitrogen AI also derives an evaluation lens from the definition during status refresh — users do not edit that lens.

## Status and confidence

Every category resolves to a **status** (green, yellow, red, or unknown) and a **confidence** level (high, medium, low, or unknown). Confidence reflects how much evidence Nitrogen AI had to work with — a green status with low confidence is a signal to add more evidence.

Each assessed category also includes a rationale, positive and negative drivers, and links back to the evidence or assessment instances it drew on.

## Refreshing status

Status recomputes automatically in the background after materials are indexed, Drive-linked files sync, assessments are approved (or approval is revoked), and variables change. The Overview polls briefly to pick up the new scores. You can still click **Refresh** anytime for an on-demand recompute (for example after editing a category definition).

## Overriding a status

If you disagree with the system-computed status, you can override it with your own status and an explanation. Overrides always take precedence and are recorded with who made them and when, so the history stays auditable.

## Where it shows up

Project status appears as a compact summary on the project overview, and as a full table with source links and edit controls in the expanded project view. See [Manage project status](/guides/manage-project-status) for how to edit categories and override a status.
