title: "Project status"
description: "Project status tracks a project's health across editable categories, each with an AI-computed status, confidence, and rationale."
---

**Project status** gives you an at-a-glance view of how healthy a project is, broken into categories you can customize. Each category is assessed by AI against your project's evidence, materials, and assessment outputs, and can be manually overridden when you know better than the model.

## Default categories

Every new project starts with five editable categories:

- **Evidence & credibility** — material claims are supported by traceable sources and there are no major contradictions in the project record
- **Technical viability** — the proposed design and modeled outputs are coherent for the site and use case
- **Funding & economics** — cost, revenue, and funding logic hang together and the economic case is directionally credible
- **Deployment readiness** — a credible path to build and operate, with named owners and no unresolved blockers
- **Risk profile** — material risks are identified, ownership is understood, and mitigation paths exist for the most important items

Each category has an editable **definition** describing what "healthy" means for that category on your project.

## Status and confidence

Every category resolves to a **status** (green, yellow, red, or unknown) and a **confidence** level (high, medium, low, or unknown). Confidence reflects how much evidence Nitrogen AI had to work with — a green status with low confidence is a signal to add more evidence.

Each assessed category also includes a rationale, positive and negative drivers, and links back to the evidence or assessment instances it drew on.

## Refreshing status

Status doesn't update automatically on every change. Refresh a category manually, or refresh all categories at once, after adding evidence or completing an assessment.

## Overriding a status

If you disagree with the system-computed status, you can override it with your own status and an explanation. Overrides always take precedence and are recorded with who made them and when, so the history stays auditable.

## Where it shows up

Project status appears as a compact summary on the project overview, and as a full table with source links and edit controls in the expanded project view. See [Manage project status](/guides/manage-project-status) for how to edit categories and override a status.
