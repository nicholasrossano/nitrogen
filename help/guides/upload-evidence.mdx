---
title: 'Upload evidence'
description: 'Add PDFs, Word documents, or Excel files to your project so Nitrogen AI can retrieve relevant passages and cite your materials in every deliverable.'
---

Evidence is the source material that powers everything Nitrogen AI generates for your project. When you upload a document, the platform reads it, splits it into passages, and indexes those passages for semantic search. When you run an assessment, the AI retrieves the most relevant content from your files and cites it directly in the output.

## Supported file formats

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="PDF" icon="file-pdf">
    Feasibility studies, technical reports, environmental assessments, policy documents. Up to 50 MB per file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DOCX" icon="file-word">
    Project proposals, narrative reports, due diligence write-ups, partner agreements. Up to 50 MB per file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="XLSX / XLS" icon="file-excel">
    Financial models, energy data, survey results, cost breakdowns. Up to 50 MB per file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PPTX" icon="file-powerpoint">
    Investor decks, project overview presentations. Up to 50 MB per file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pages / Keynote" icon="file">
    Apple Pages documents and Keynote presentations. Up to 50 MB per file.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Only PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, XLS, Pages, and Keynote files are accepted for evidence. Files in other formats will fail processing. If your content is in an unsupported format, convert it first or paste the text directly.
</Warning>

## Upload a file to your project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    From your dashboard, click on the project you want to add evidence to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Files view">
    Open the **Files** view from the project navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Drag a file onto the upload area, or click **Upload file** to browse. You can upload multiple files — repeat for each document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    After the file is accepted, Nitrogen AI indexes the content for semantic search. Most files process in under a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Evidence uploads are rate-limited to 120 per minute per user.
</Note>

## Paste text instead of uploading a file

If your content isn't in a file, click **Paste text** in the evidence widget, give it a descriptive title, paste the content, and click **Add**. Pasted text is processed identically to an uploaded file.

## What to upload

Upload a mix of document types to give the AI a fuller picture of your project. The most valuable evidence types are:

- **Feasibility studies** — pre-feasibility and full feasibility studies typically contain technical, financial, and social data in one document
- **Technical reports** — engineering assessments, grid studies, energy audits, and resource assessments
- **Financial models** — Excel-based cash flow models, tariff analyses, and cost breakdowns
- **Stakeholder and social data** — community assessments, beneficiary surveys, and social baseline reports

<Tip>
  You don't need a perfect set of documents before running assessments. Start with what you have and add more evidence over time to improve subsequent generations.
</Tip>

## Manage your evidence

All uploaded files and pasted text appear in the **Evidence** tab of your project. To remove a document, click the trash icon next to it. Deletion removes the document record and all of its indexed passages and cannot be undone.

<Warning>
  Deleting evidence does not retroactively affect assessment outputs that have already been generated. It only prevents the deleted content from being used in future generation runs.
</Warning>
