# Brand and naming

Forks, self-hosted deployments, and custom assessments are welcome. The [AGPLv3 license](LICENSE) covers Nitrogen AI's source code and is the license you use when you run, modify, or redistribute that code.

The AGPLv3 does **not** grant rights to the **Nitrogen AI** project name, logo, or branding as a product identity. That keeps it clear which project is the upstream and which is a fork.

**You are encouraged to:** fork the repo, rename your deployment, customize assessments and workflows, and say accurately that your work is "based on Nitrogen AI" or "compatible with Nitrogen AI."

**Please don't:** use Nitrogen AI (or bare "Nitrogen") as the primary name of your fork or product, register domains or social handles that look like the official project, or imply endorsement or affiliation you don't have.
