# Brand and naming

Forks, self-hosted deployments, and custom assessments are welcome. The [AGPLv3 license](LICENSE) covers Nitrogen's source code and is the license you use when you run, modify, or redistribute that code.

The AGPLv3 does **not** grant rights to the **Nitrogen** project name, logo, or branding as a product identity. That keeps it clear which project is the upstream and which is a fork.

**You are encouraged to:** fork the repo, rename your deployment, customize assessments and workflows, and say accurately that your work is "based on Nitrogen" or "compatible with Nitrogen."

**Please don't:** use Nitrogen as the primary name of your fork or product, register domains or social handles that look like the official project, or imply endorsement or affiliation you don't have.
